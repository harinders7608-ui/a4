Artisan Disclaimer

Review the formal institutional commitments, client privileges, and operational protocols governing CrocodileTote.

1. Section 1 Provisions

The leathercraft treatises, tensile load calculations, and hide care recommendations published across CrocodileTote are presented solely for educational enrichment and historical interest. While our workshop conducts empirical stress tests and tensile evaluations, hide longevity involves diverse environmental factors, humidity exposure, and variable maintenance routines. Patrons rely upon our leather care guides, tensile estimates, and conditioning protocols entirely at their independent personal discretion.

2. Section 2 Provisions

Manual leathercraft, edge beveling, diamond awl piercing, and leather skiving demand specialized sharp hand tools, ergonomic posture, calibrated manual force, and supportive workshop routines. Informational materials published on this website do not replace certified occupational safety training in traditional cordwaining craft. CrocodileTote disclaims legal liability for personal injury, blade accidents, or material damage sustained while attempting referenced benchwork methodologies across workshops.

3. Section 3 Provisions

Digital representations, photographic imagery, and color swatches presented across our online catalog are calibrated to reflect actual products as faithfully as possible. However, because natural vegetable-tanned calfskin absorbs natural oils uniquely, minor discrepancies in shade and grain texture will inevitably occur. Ambient lighting conditions and monitor screen calibrations influence perceived tones. Patrons are encouraged to inspect sample cuttings at our Manhattan studio.

4. Section 4 Provisions

This website may contain hypertext links directing visitors to third-party tannery guilds, historical museum collections, or independent toolmakers not operated by CrocodileTote. Such external references are provided solely as informational resources, and our organization does not endorse or assume responsibility for external web content. Visitors accessing third-party internet locations do so under the distinct terms and privacy protocols established by those independent operators.

5. Section 5 Provisions

For formal clarification regarding any workshop documentation, material provenance disclosures, or artisan accreditation standards, please submit written communication directly to our administrative office. You may direct inquiries to CrocodileTote Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or contact our patron advisory desk directly by telephone at +1-888-777-5845. We remain dedicated to providing transparent, verifiable craft information to all patrons and leather connoisseurs.
